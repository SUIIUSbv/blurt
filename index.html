Blurt it out.
Clean transcripts, nothing else.

Blurt is a Chrome extension that turns the captions of the video you are watching into clean, readable text. One click, runs locally in your browser, no account, no tracking.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store
  • Clean text by defaultCaption fragments become paragraphs. No [Music] tags, no repeated lines.
  • Five formatsPlain text, Markdown, SRT, WebVTT and JSON. Copy or save as a file.
  • Ready for your notesMarkdown with frontmatter and chapters as headings, for Obsidian or Notion.
  • Pick the chaptersExport only the parts you need, with their original timestamps.
  • Copy shortcutAlt+Shift+C (Option+Shift+C on Mac) copies without opening anything.
  • Private by designTranscripts never leave your browser. No analytics, no account.
